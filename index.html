Solar PV in Luxembourg, municipality by municipality

805.6 MW of solar PV connected as of 14 September 2026, up +36% since the Q1 2025 reading: solar accounts for 64.7% of decentralised generation capacity. The Open Data portal asked for the share of solar PV in electricity production by municipality; since the Leneda files give connected capacity rather than energy produced, this dashboard measures the PV share of installed capacity, municipality by municipality.

Method & source

Data: the “Electricity generation in Luxembourg” dataset published by Leneda on data.public.lu (CC0 licence): quarterly readings Q1 2025 → Q1 2026 and the weekly reading of 14 September 2026 (municipality, canton and per-installation files).

The figures describe installed capacity (kW connected) of the decentralised generation fleet, not the energy produced (kWh): the real share of PV in delivered electricity depends on sunshine and load factors. “Δ kW since Q1 2025” compares the current reading with the first published quarterly reading. In the per-municipality table, only photovoltaic capacity is broken out; the “Total kW” column aggregates all technologies.
