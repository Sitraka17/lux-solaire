Solar PV in Luxembourg, municipality by municipality

805.6 MW of solar PV connected as of 14 September 2026, up +36% since the Q1 2025 reading: solar accounts for 64.7% of decentralised generation capacity. The Open Data portal asked for the share of solar PV in electricity production by municipality; since the Leneda files give connected capacity rather than energy produced, this dashboard measures the PV share of installed capacity, municipality by municipality.

What share of electricity comes from solar?

Capacity (MW) and production (GWh) are two different measures. According to the ILR (Key figures 2025 of the electricity market, published 2026), solar PV produced 627 GWh in 2025, ahead of wind (467 GWh), out of a national production of 1,826 GWh and a gross national consumption of 6,529 GWh; 444 GWh were self-consumed or shared (7% of final consumption). Lux-solaire calculation from these figures: PV accounts for 34.3% of national production and 9.6% of gross consumption.

Official reference figures

ILR, end of 2025: 746 MW of solar PV (+197 MW, +35.8%) and 33,304 installations (+10,694, +47.3%). Leneda, Q4 2025 reading: 745.4 MW and 33,250 installations.

Which municipalities have the most solar PV per inhabitant?

RNPP population as of 1er July 2026

Set against population, PV capacity reverses the rankings: 1,161 W per inhabitant nationally (municipal median 1,653 W/inh.), 7,887 W/inh. in Colmar-Berg against 190 in Luxembourg. Method & source

  
Data: the “Electricity generation in Luxembourg” dataset published by Leneda on data.public.lu (CC0 licence): quarterly readings Q1 2025 → Q1 2026 and the weekly reading of 14 September 2026 (municipality, canton and per-installation files).

  
The figures describe installed capacity (kW connected) of the decentralised generation fleet, not the energy produced (kWh): the real share of PV in delivered electricity depends on sunshine and load factors. “Δ kW since Q1 2025” compares the current reading with the first published quarterly reading. In the per-municipality table, only photovoltaic capacity is broken out; the “Total kW” column aggregates all technologies.

  
Population: National Register of Natural Persons (RNPP) as of 1er July 2026, 693,913 residents, CC0 dataset on data.public.lu; this administrative register differs from STATEC's official population estimate. “W/inh.” = PV kW × 1,000 / population; “rooftops” = PV installations of 30 kW or less.

  
The data derived on this page (tables, CSV and JSON) are published under the CC BY 4.0 licence; the Leneda and RNPP source files remain under CC0.
