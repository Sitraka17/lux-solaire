Large installations: rows that appeared

over 100 kW, from one archived reading to the next

In Wincrange, Leneda's per-installation file lists 49 installations over 100 kW as of the 9 November 2026 reading, all technologies (49 on 14 September 2026).

No row over 100 kW appeared or disappeared in this municipality between 14 September 2026 and 9 November 2026.

Method. Every week Leneda publishes a file with one row per installation: municipality, technology and installed capacity, with no identifier, no commissioning date and no operator. It replaces this file without keeping any history; lux-solaire archives a copy at each reading. Without an identifier, an installation cannot be followed from one reading to the next: rows over 100 kW (exactly 100 kW excluded) of two consecutive copies are compared municipality by municipality and technology by technology. A row that appears may be a new installation, but also an installation extended beyond 100 kW, a corrected value or a row assigned to another municipality; the file does not say which. Copies compared: 14 September 2026, 26 October 2026 (42 days after the previous one), 2 November 2026, 9 November 2026.
