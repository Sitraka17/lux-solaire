Solar panels in Luxembourg: are they worth it for your home?

Tool 1: how much your roof would produce and earn each year. Tool 2: how many years until the system pays for itself, with the 2026 Klimabonus or with the 15-year guaranteed feed-in tariff, and which of the two suits you. Everything is computed in your browser from official sources: yield from PVGIS, subsidies from guichet.lu, ILR grid tariffs, the grand-ducal regulation on feed-in tariffs. Benchmark: 38,066 installations are already connected in Luxembourg (26 October 2026).

Frequently asked questionsWhat people in Luxembourg ask before installing

How much does 1 kWp of solar panels produce in Luxembourg?

About 1,070 kWh a year for a due-south roof pitched at 35° in Luxembourg City (PVGIS, 2005-2023 average, 14% system losses), 1,010 kWh for a south-east or south-west roof, 860 kWh for an east or west roof. The north of the country (Clervaux, Wiltz) produces 3% less, the south (Esch, Capellen) 2% more. A 6 kWp system therefore produces 5,500 to 6,400 kWh a year, 1.4 to 1.6 times the consumption of an average household (3,900 kWh).

How much is the Klimabonus for solar PV in 2026?

For a self-consumption system of power P (in kWp, at least 2), the subsidy is P × (1,155 − 33 × P) euros below 15 kWp and 10,000 euros from 15 kWp: €3,168 for 3 kWp, €5,742 for 6 kWp, €8,250 for 10 kWp. A battery of capacity Q (kWh) adds Q × (500 − 500/18 × Q) euros, €2,250 from 9 kWh. The subsidy is deducted from the invoice by the installer (pre-financing) for invoices dated 4 January 2026 to 31 December 2029. Condition: waive any guaranteed remuneration for the electricity fed in.

Klimabonus or guaranteed feed-in tariff: which to choose?

The two cannot be combined. The Klimabonus cuts the upfront investment and suits small or medium systems that self-consume a good share of their output. The guaranteed tariff (13.3 c€/kWh for a first feed-in in 2026, 12.9 c€ in 2027, for 15 years, up to 10 kW) pays better for the surplus and becomes competitive when the system is large relative to consumption. Tool 2 computes both for your case; as a rule, the more you feed in, the closer option B gets to option A.

How much does a solar PV system cost in Luxembourg?

2026 quotes seen from installers range from €1,600 to €2,200 per kWp installed for 6 to 15 kWp, i.e. €10,000 to €13,000 for 6 kWp, VAT included. Supply and installation on a dwelling qualifies for the super-reduced VAT rate of 3%. After the Klimabonus, a 6 kWp system costs about €4,500 to €7,500.

What is a self-consumed kWh worth?

Only the variable components of the bill are avoided: energy (about 13 c€/kWh in 2026), the grid's volumetric charge (5.10 c€), the compensation mechanism (−3.71 c€ in 2026, thanks to the State's €150 million contribution), the electricity tax (0.10 c€), all subject to 8% VAT: about 16 c€/kWh in 2026, and close to 20 c€ if the State contribution ends. Fixed charges (metering, reference power) remain due. Counting 25 c€ because "my bill divided by my kWh gives 25 cents" overstates the gain.

Do you need a battery?

A battery lifts the self-consumed share from about 30% to 50-60% for a typical house, but costs €700 to €1,100 per kWh installed, part of which the Klimabonus refunds (up to €2,250). With a surplus sold at 5 c€ and an avoided kWh worth 20 c€, each stored kWh earns 15 c€: a 7 kWh battery cycled 250 times a year earns about €260 a year for €4,000 to €6,000 of net investment. It therefore usually lengthens the payback time; tool 2 shows it for your case.

Is the income from a solar PV system taxable?

For a private individual operating a system of 1 to 30 kWp, the direct tax administration accepts, as a matter of tolerance, that it is a hobby activity not counted in taxable income (circular L.I.R. no. 14/2 of 5 June 2023, summarised by Klima-Agence). With pure self-consumption there is no commercial activity at all.

How do I check my roof's potential?

The national solar cadastre (energie.geoportail.lu) rates every roof in the country by its potential; Klima-Agence offers free, neutral advice. For a fine estimate, PVGIS accepts the exact address, shading and actual orientation.

Method and sources

  
This calculator is a decision aid, not a quote: it knows neither your roof's shading, nor your hourly consumption profile, nor your contract. It sells nothing and sends no data: everything is computed in your browser. The assumptions are displayed, adjustable and dated; please report any regulatory change to us.

  
Calculation. Production = power × PVGIS yield (orientation, pitch) × canton factor, spread by month along the PVGIS profile for Luxembourg City. Self-consumed share without battery = 30% × (production ÷ consumption)−0.6, bounded between 10 and 90%; a battery of Q kWh adds 35% × (1 − e−Q ÷ (0.5 × daily consumption)). Annual gain = self-consumed kWh × value of the avoided kWh (indexed by the chosen rise) + kWh fed in × surplus price (option A) or guaranteed tariff for 15 years then surplus price (option B). Production declines at the chosen ageing rate; the inverter is replaced in year 13. Payback time = first year in which the cumulative cash flow turns positive (interpolated); net gain = cumulative cash flow at 25 years in nominal euros; IRR = rate that sets the present value of the flows to zero. No maintenance or insurance cost is counted.
